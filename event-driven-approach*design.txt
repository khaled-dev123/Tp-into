Event-Driven Architecture for Internship Management


The main components are:

Department Head: Initiates the internship request.
Faculty: Prepares and sends the internship agreement.
Company: Receives and signs the documents.
Finance Department: Processes the payment.
Event Broker: Distributes events between services.
DMS: Stores all documents for future reference.
Notification Service: Sends notifications to concerned users.
2. Workflow and Events
Request creation: The Head of Department sends an internship request to the company. The event InternshipRequestCreated is generated, and the document is stored in the DMS.
Request signed: The company signs the request. The event InternshipRequestSigned is generated, triggering the Faculty to prepare the internship agreement.
Agreement creation: The Faculty prepares the internship agreement, stores it in the DMS, and sends it to the company. The event InternshipAgreementCreated is generated.
Agreement signed: The company signs the agreement. The event InternshipAgreementSigned is generated and sent to the Finance Department.
Payment: Finance retrieves the signed agreement from the DMS and processes the payment. The event PaymentProcessed is generated.

All documents are stored in the DMS throughout the process.
