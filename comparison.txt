Comparison of the Two Architectures

1. Coupling

Workflow Orchestration: Higher coupling because all services depend on a central orchestrator.
Event-Driven Architecture: Lower coupling because services communicate independently through events.

Winner: Event-Driven Architecture.

2. Scalability

Workflow Orchestration: Moderate scalability because the central orchestrator may become a bottleneck.
Event-Driven Architecture: High scalability because each service can scale independently.

Winner: Event-Driven Architecture.

3. Fault Tolerance

Workflow Orchestration: If the orchestrator fails, the entire workflow may stop until it recovers.
Event-Driven Architecture: If one service fails, other services can continue working, and events can be processed later.

Winner: Event-Driven Architecture.
